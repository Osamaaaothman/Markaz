# ADR 0004 — ZATCA e-invoicing: integrate directly, no solution provider

Status: **accepted by Osama, 2026-10-07** (resolves `docs/01-OPEN-DECISIONS.md` A4).
Scope: the Saudi compliance pack (`packages/compliance/zatca-sa`) behind the country-agnostic contract
(`docs/06-COMPLIANCE-PACK-RULES.md`).

## Decision

Each customer deployment talks to ZATCA's Fatoora platform **directly**, with its own keys and certificates. No
solution provider or middleware sits between the product and ZATCA. A provider adapter may be added later behind the same
`ICompliancePack` contract for a customer who asks for one.

## Why

- The product is sold as a **one-time purchase, one isolated deployment per customer** (ADR 0002). A per-customer
  provider subscription (typically 99-999 SAR per month plus VAT, from vendor price pages, not from a quote) breaks that
  model and moves the customer's invoices to a third party.
- ZATCA does not charge for its APIs as far as we have found (not confirmed in an official document).
- Direct costs us engineering time once (our estimate: 3-6 weeks, not sourced) instead of a recurring fee per customer.

## What was verified, and when (2026-10-07)

Read in full from the official documents downloaded from zatca.gov.sa (copies are not committed; see "Sources"):

- **E-invoicing Detailed Technical Guideline, Version 2, Nov 2022** (81 pages). Confirms: the taxpayer must make sure the
  EGS solution is compliant; onboarding = OTP generated by the taxpayer in the FATOORA portal -> CSR from the EGS ->
  compliance CSID -> compliance checks -> production CSID; CSIDs renew and can be revoked; a CSID is a certificate used
  to sign **simplified (B2C)** invoices and to authenticate to the Reporting and Clearance APIs; the SDK
  ("Compliance and Enablement Toolbox") and the Integration Sandbox are **optional** tools. The text lists no rule that
  a solution must be bought from an approved vendor. (Blog posts that say otherwise were not confirmed by ZATCA text.)
- **Security Features Implementation Standards v1.2, 2023-05-19** (27 pages). Confirms: ECDSA with SHA-256; XAdES
  enveloped signature, level B-B, covering the whole XML except the QR element; PKCS#10 CSR with the listed subject
  fields (VAT number as organizationIdentifier, EGS serial `1-...|2-...|3-...`, invoice types `TSCZ`); keys must be
  non-exportable and protected; certificate valid up to 5 years; CRLs valid 7 days (so an EGS can work offline for up to
  7 days before it must refresh the CRL); standard (B2B) invoices are cleared and stamped by ZATCA, simplified invoices
  are stamped by our EGS.
- **XML Implementation Standard** (72 pages) downloaded; **not yet read** for field-level rules.

## Not verified yet (blocks the matching work, do not build from memory)

1. **Which curve.** The security standard says "P-256" for the certificate key; ZATCA's own SDK and tooling are widely
   reported to use secp256k1. Decide from the SDK and sandbox, not from either claim.
2. The **API contract** (endpoints, headers, request and response bodies): the Integration Sandbox swagger is not
   reachable by our tools (HTTP 403); it has to come from the developer portal in a browser.
3. The **current wave schedule** and whether newer versions of the three documents above exist (the technical guideline
   is dated Nov 2022).
4. **Clearance when the internet is down.** Simplified invoices may be reported within 24 hours (to be confirmed in the
   current text); standard invoices need an online clearance before they are valid.
5. Whether the sandbox needs a registered developer account.

## Shape of the build (contract-first, nothing leaves the pack)

1. `packages/compliance/contract`: `ICompliancePack`, `ComplianceOutcome` (CLEARED | REPORTED | ACCEPTED_WITH_WARNINGS |
   REJECTED | PENDING_RETRY | PACK_UNAVAILABLE), types. No country logic.
2. `packages/compliance/zatca-sa`: UBL 2.1 XML builder, hash and previous-invoice-hash chain, signature (XAdES B-B),
   QR (TLV), CSR and onboarding client, Reporting and Clearance client. Pure parts first, with golden-file tests; checked
   against ZATCA's own SDK validator.
3. Outbox worker submits after the invoice and its ledger entry commit (never inside the transaction), with bounded
   retries and a dead-letter state that alerts. State machine:
   `DRAFT -> PENDING -> SUBMITTING -> CLEARED | REPORTED | ACCEPTED_WITH_WARNINGS | REJECTED | FAILED_RETRYABLE`.
4. Keys: generated in the deployment, never leave it, stored encrypted with a key held outside the database, never
   logged, never returned by an API, every access audited. Renewal and revocation designed from the start.
5. Sandbox and simulation mode is stored on the tenant record and shown in the UI and on every invoice, so nobody can
   mistake a test invoice for a real one.
