Markaz — AI assistant “orb” · design reference

Markaz is an Arabic/English accounting ERP for Saudi and Gulf companies. The assistant lives in a side panel and a full page, and talks to the books (sales, stock, payroll, reports). This page shows the brand, where the orb appears, the seven states it must express, and the current v1 (which we want to replace with something better).

v1 below is functional but not good enough: it feels static, it is not interactive, and at small sizes the states are hard to tell apart. Treat it as a starting point, not a constraint.

The emblem: four corner brackets framing a brass square — “markaz” means “centre”. The app’s loading screen draws the brackets in, pops the centre, then a light shine passes over the tile. No colour gradients anywhere (flat colours only); the app supports light and dark themes and several “design directions”, so neutral colours come from CSS variables.
